# Use the ticker's `buy` field to price BTC sales, `sell` to price purchases

The edital explicitly says purchases (RF07) convert at the "cotação de venda" (`sell`). It says nothing explicit about which field prices a sale (RF09). Manual testing of the live Mercado Bitcoin ticker (2026-09-08, two reads six minutes apart) confirmed `buy`/`sell` move independently and consistently with `last`/`vol` — real bid/ask data, not a static mock. Standard exchange-ticker convention is `buy` = the price a market maker pays to buy (bid), `sell` = the price it charges to sell (ask). By that convention, and by symmetry with RF07 (the platform sells BTC to the client at `sell`), the platform buys BTC back from the client — a client sale — at `buy`. This is an inference from market convention plus live-endpoint evidence, not an explicit sentence in the edital.
